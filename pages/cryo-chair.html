Cryo Chair™

The Cryo Chair is a portable chair with a series of gel capsules designed to cool the player. It cools the back, buttocks, armpits and neck — two of the most effective areas to reduce body temperature. The chair can be sat on normally or laid flat on the ground for players who need to lie down.

Key Benefits

  • Cools the back, buttocks, armpits and neck of the player.
  • Portable and easy to transport.
  • Can be sat on or laid flat on the ground.
  • Targets the most effective cooling zones to rapidly reduce core temperature and reduce the chance of heat exhaustion and heat stroke.

How The Cryo Chair Compares

There is no associated product on the market. The Cryo Chair is the only portable cooling chair of its kind — purpose-built for athletes and patients who need rapid, targeted cooling in the field.

Excellent For

  • Keeping athletes cool on the bench
  • Heat Stroke
  • Heat Exhaustion
