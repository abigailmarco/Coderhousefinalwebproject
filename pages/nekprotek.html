NekProTek™

The NekProTek™ is manufactured utilizing high-impact foam and coated with vinyl for easy cleaning. The collar attaches and removes easily with Velcro, to any helmet, or it can be worn independently — providing 360-degree energy impact protection from skates, sticks, pucks, and falls. Includes a throat protector designed specifically for hockey players.

NekProTek™ (Cycling)

Key Benefits

Protects the base of the skull and cervical spine. Hooks directly into the harness inside your bicycle helmet for a secure, low-profile fit. Doctor recommended — most critical and fatal injuries happen below the helmet line, usually at the base of the skull.

How NekProTek Compares

There is no comparable product on the market. The closest thing athletes currently use is a simple Kevlar sleeve — designed only to protect from cuts. It offers no impact protection, and the store manager at Pure Hockey notes these sleeves tend to lose their shape over time, reducing even that cut protection. NekProTek™ is the only product that protects from both cuts and impact — and holds its shape.

Why It Works

The design is based on a simple principle — by placing energy-absorbing high-impact foam around the neck, you reduce the chance of injury to the neck and base of the skull. Protection that is both logical and effective.
