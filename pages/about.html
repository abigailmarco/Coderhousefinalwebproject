About Us

How It Started

As a medic with the Chicago Fire Department, I treated thousands of patients with traumatic brain injuries. Through those experiences, I designed and patented a solution that can dramatically reduce Traumatic Brain Injury, Heat Stroke, and potentially Chronic Traumatic Encephalopathy (CTE).

Our Mission

Our products are state-of-the-art preventive injury solutions, designed to reduce the chance of injury and treat the injured player in the event of injury. By connecting the dots of research papers from around the globe, we bring cutting-edge, science-backed protection to athletes, workers, and families at every level.

Bob's Research

Bob spent years researching why NFL, NCAA, and High School players suffer so many traumatic brain injuries. After reviewing hundreds of studies, he concluded that the heat inside a helmet — as high as 110°F — causes the brain to swell, making it far more susceptible to traumatic brain injury, greater injury severity, and longer recovery. He also indicates that heat is a major factor in Chronic Traumatic Encephalopathy.
