Our Products

The Vortex™ Helmet — Helmet Liner

The Vortex™ Helmet reduces the chance of brain swelling (102°F), reduces the chance of Traumatic Brain Injuries (TBI) and Heat Stroke. It absorbs energy better than any foam-lined helmet in the industry, especially in high heat and humidity. Designed for all athletes in any sport.

NekProTek™

Manufactured utilizing high-impact foam and coated with vinyl for easy cleaning, NekProTek provides 360-degree energy impact protection from skates, sticks, pucks, and falls. Features a throat protector designed specifically for hockey players. Attaches and removes easily with Velcro.

Learn More

Cryo Chair™

A portable chair with a series of gel capsules designed to cool the back, buttocks, armpits and neck of the player — two of the most effective areas to reduce body temperature. Can be sat on or laid flat on the ground for players who need to lie down.
